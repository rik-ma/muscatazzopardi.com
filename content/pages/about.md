I'm the CEO of **Switch**, a B2B brand and marketing consultancy I run with my brother Ed. I co-founded Switch Digital in 2013; in 2016 we merged with Switch to create a single unified agency. In 2019 Ed and I bought out our two partners, and we've run it together ever since.

The part of the Switch story I'm proudest of is the transformation. We went from a 360° agency serving every kind of client in Malta to a predominantly B2B consultancy working with brands that make a difference in the world around them, and in the past four years our clients went from Malta-only to mostly the UK and the US. We did it deliberately: a four-day work week, a fully remote team, and a culture where kindness is an operating principle rather than a poster in a kitchen.

None of that is incidental. One of my main goals in life is to prove that you can run successful businesses with kindness, and that the most sustainable businesses are a positive influence on their clients, their people, their communities, and the environment they operate in. Switch is my working proof.

Alongside Switch:

- **I work as a fractional CMO and strategy consultant** with a small number of owner-led manufacturing and technology companies, mostly in the US and the UK.
- **I coach CFOs across the United States with Dope CFO**, helping them build their own practices serving the cannabis industry.
- **I've been a Vistage member since 2018 and a Vistage Chair since 2023**, spending part of every month helping a group of business leaders make better decisions and build companies worth being proud of.
- **I completed Stanford Graduate School of Business's Executive Leadership Program in 2022**, and I've guest lectured on marketing at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business since 2020.
- **I sit on the Strategic Committee of [ICOM](https://icomagencies.com)**, one of the world's largest networks of independent agencies.

Mentoring is the thread through all of it. Beyond the formal work, I regularly help people around me start and build businesses of their own, sustainably.

Before any of this, there was publishing: an internship at Allied Newspapers at seventeen, a media company co-founded at university and grown to seven titles before Malta's leading publisher acquired it, then marketing leadership roles at the Famalco Group and GFI Software. Everything I know about writing for real readers, and about how businesses actually work from the inside, started there.

Away from work, I live in Gozo with my wife Sarah, where we're slowly (oh so slowly) building a house. I write, I hike, I run (very slow marathons included; I train for them regularly, with mixed grace), and I shoot street and architecture photography. I read a lot of philosophy, with a soft spot for the existentialists and the Stoics, though I'll make the case for the Epicureans and even the hedonists over a glass of wine. And I'm a firm believer in buying for life rather than for the moment; one of my favourite conversations is how we can collectively choose better, consume better, and leave things better than we found them.

If any of that sounds like the start of a conversation, [I'd genuinely like to hear from you](/contact).
